Seasonal Profile

The Winter Palette

A masterclass in high‑contrast clarity. Winter is defined by cool, jewel‑toned saturation and icy undertones that command presence through sharp visual precision. Unlike the soft transitions of Summer, Winter is bold and uncompromising.

THE UNDERTONE

Cool & Striking Winter skin types possess blue or pink undertones. There is a total absence of golden or peach warmth, creating a vivid, crystalline clarity.

KEY VALUE

High Contrast The palette is defined by high saturation and luminescence. Vivid, high‑energy colors without grayness that command attention through depth.

KEY METALS

Platinum
Silver

Polished finishes and high‑reflectivity textures in cool metals best reflect the sharp, luminous quality of Winter.

The Sub-Season Spectrum

01 / SHADOW CORE

Deep Winter

Leaning towards the shadow, this palette features dark, intense colors like charcoal, burgundy, and deep plum.

02 / PURE FROST

True Winter

The core of the season. Pure, cool, and high‑contrast. Think key blue, stark white, and cobalt—crisp and uncompromising.

03 / LUMINOUS EDGE

Bright Winter

The intersection of Winter and Spring. High saturation and luminescence. Fuchsia and electric lime blaze with clarity.

Clarity Level: Maximum

Winter Neutrals

The foundation of high‑contrast clarity. Pure black and stark white provide the essential anchor for Winter accents.
