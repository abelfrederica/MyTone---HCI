Seasonal Profile

The Summer Palette

Embodying the gentle transitions of hazy afternoon sunlight, the Summer palette is defined by cool blue‑based undertones and a soft, muted clarity. Unlike the sharp contrast of Winter, Summer colors are sophisticated, washed‑out, and elegantly understated.

THE UNDERTONE

Blue-Based Cool Summer skin has a distinctly cool, pinkish, or blue undertone. There is a delicate quality to the complexion that thrives in refined, non‑vibrant hues.

KEY VALUE

Soft & Muted Colours are never neon or primary; they are always “grayed down.” Think of colors viewed through a fine silk veil or a gentle summer mist.

KEY METALS

Silver
White Gold

Brushed finishes and matte textures in cool metals complement the muted Summer essence better than high‑shine yellow gold.

The Sub-Season Spectrum

01 / EARLY MORNING

Light Summer

The brightest of the Summers. High in lightness, bordering on Spring but maintaining a strictly cool core. Think iced lemonade and sky blue.

02 / HIGH NOON

True Summer

The heart of the season. Pure cool undertones with medium saturation. Defined by raspberry, spruce, and classic chambray.

03 / TWILIGHT

Soft Summer

The bridge to Autumn. The most muted Summer palette. Earthier, featuring charcoal, sage green, and soft burgundy.

Mutedness Level: High

Summer Neutrals

Essential base tones for a refined Summer wardrobe.
