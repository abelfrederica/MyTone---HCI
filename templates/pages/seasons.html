Understanding Your Seasonal Palette

Color analysis is the precise study of how natural lighting interacts with your unique biological pigments. By categorizing harmony into four distinct seasons, we unlock a wardrobe that illuminates your natural features.

Spring

Warm & Bright

Defined by golden undertones. Clear, vibrant colors that enhance natural warmth in the skin and hair.

Summer

Cool & Muted

Soft, blue-based tones. Gentle, dusty colors that create a serene and elegant glow.

Autumn

Warm & Muted

Rich and earthy ochres, olives, and terracottas that provide a grounded, sophisticated frame.

Winter

Cool & Bright

High contrast jewel tones. Stark blacks, icy silvers, and royal purples for bold expression.
