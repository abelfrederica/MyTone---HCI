Seasonal Profile

The Autumn Palette

Autumn is the season of depth and resonance. It captures the essence of a sun‑drenched forest floor, defined by golden undertones and a rich, muted saturation that exudes grounded luxury and warmth.

THE UNDERTONE

Warm Earthy Autumn skin radiates with golden‑yellow or red undertones. The complexion has a characteristic warmth that is enhanced by rich, organic colors.

KEY VALUE

Rich & Muted Colors are distinguished by their addition of black or gray to warm hues, creating a lower value that mimics the natural richness of the harvest season.

KEY METALS

Copper
Bronze

Antiqued and hammered finishes in warm metallics like brass and copper harmonize perfectly with the season’s deep tones.

The Sub-Season Spectrum

01 / EARLY HARVEST

Soft Autumn

The bridge between Summer and Autumn. Muted, gentle, and hazy. Think of olive greens, toasted almonds, and sand.

02 / PEAK SEASON

Warm Autumn

The true heart of the season. Vibrant but grounded. Golden browns, pumpkin spice, and mustard yellows.

03 / LATE HARVEST

Deep Autumn

The transition into Winter. High contrast and heavy value. Deep forest greens and rich burgundies.

Saturation Level: Rich

Autumn Neutrals

Essential foundation tones for a grounded Autumn wardrobe. Favor warm beiges, rich chocolates, and earthy greens.
