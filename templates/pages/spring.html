Seasonal Profile

The Spring Palette

Defined by warmth and luminosity, the Spring season represents a rebirth of color. It is characterized by clear, vibrant hues that carry a distinct golden undertone, mirroring the awakening of nature after winter.

THE UNDERTONE

Warm Golden Undertones The primary driver of the Spring palette is warmth. Every color contains a hint of gold, creating a glow that harmonizes with skin that has yellow or peachy undertones.

KEY VALUE

Clear & Vibrant Unlike Autumn’s muted tones, Spring is high‑chroma. The colors are crisp, energetic, and free from grayish or muddy influences.

KEY METALS

Yellow Gold
Rose Gold

Polished or brushed 14k–18k gold elevates the natural warmth of the Spring complexion better than cool silvers.

The Sub-Season Spectrum

01 / EARLY MORNING

Clear Spring

The highest contrast subtype. Characterized by sparkling eyes and vibrant skin that needs intense, saturated colors like bright poppy and turquoise.

02 / HIGH NOON

Warm Spring

The true heart of Spring. Focuses on medium‑intensity colors with strong golden influences. Think apricot, honey, and warm greens.

03 / TWILIGHT

Light Spring

The softest of the trio. Transitions toward Summer. Colors are delicate and airy, like peach fuzz, light aqua, and soft yellow.
